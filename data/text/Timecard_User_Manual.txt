Preface:
A timecard is the process of recording and tracking the number of hours an employee has contributed to a specific project or set of tasks.
Pre-requisites for initiating a Timecard Submission :
.
The employee must have the timecard access on their menu to apply for the time sheet if the employee does not have the timecard, then they must reach out to the IA team to enable the flag for the timecard.
Ensure there is Bio or regularization details are available prior to applying time entry. Bio entries are available when swiping in and out in a day and regularization are applied if worked remotely

Note: To default the timecard details as entered earlier if any, Associate can select the ‘Last Timecard’ and click ‘Apply’ template the details of the previous timecard will be populated, Associate can modify as required.
