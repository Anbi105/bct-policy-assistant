A. Preface:
This manual serves as a comprehensive guide to assist the Associates in the respective claim process, it outlines the necessary sequential steps to be completed and the due Approval flow, unto claim settlement.
B. Pre-requisites for initiating a Claim:
.
Ensure you are eligible as per the policy to avail any specific type of claim, for more details on eligibility please review the claim policy/contact BU HR for any clarifications.
Necessary claim details are in place including proof to be attached/uploaded with the claim.
Project related claims have to be claimed only against a project, ensure you are mapped/allocated to that project.
