A. Preface:
Attendance regularization is process performed by Associates to correct /justify their attendance shortfall when compared to the expected work hours.
ATTENDANCE REGULARIZATION OPTIONS:
Regularization
Work From Home
On-site
Comp-off
Forgot ID Card
Missed to Swipe
Regularization:
Regularization Request should be performed by associates with due justification, for shortfall in Bio-metric hours / attendance hours recorded is less than ‘9’.
Work from home:
Work from home is to be Applied by associates as a request to perform their official duties remotely/ from their home.
On-site:
On site to be applied if the Associate is working from client location, which is different from their base office. Bio-metric attendance is not applicable when the Associate is on an approved-on site assignment.
Forgot-id card:
When Associates forgets to bring the Access Card/ID Card when reporting to office for a specific date, then Forgot id card to be used to Regularize Bio-metric details.
Missed to Swipe:
Missed to swipe is to be applied by Associate, when either ‘In-time’ or ‘Out-time’ swipe was missed by the Associates., but not both.
B.  Pre-requisites:
Regularization:
Biometric attendance duration should be less than 9 hours.
Minimum Biometric attendance should be greater than 7 hours to apply regularization.
Regularization should be performed within 15 days.
Work from home, Forgot id card and On-site:
For work from home, forgot id card and On-site bio metric attendance will not be available. If available, then the associate will not be eligible for these three types of requests.
Missed to swipe:
Missed to swipe can be applied, only if the associate has either a bio-metric in or Bio-metric out time.
